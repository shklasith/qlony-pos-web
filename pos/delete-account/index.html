Delete QLONY POS account and data

Use this page to start a request to permanently delete your QLONY POS account and associated account data.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. After your request is verified and completed, your QLONY POS account and applicable server-side account data cannot be restored.

What may be deleted

  • QLONY POS authentication/account records.
  • License or entitlement records linked to the account.
  • Registered-device records linked to the account.
  • Other QLONY-controlled server-side account information associated with the account.

What may not be automatically deleted

  • Copies of backups that you previously exported outside the QLONY POS app.
  • Data that must be retained for legitimate legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes, where applicable.

How deletion works

  1. Submit the request below using the Google account email associated with QLONY POS.
  2. QLONY may contact you to verify ownership of the account.
  3. After verification, the applicable account and associated server-side data will be deleted.

This page does not delete your account instantly. Your identity must be verified before deletion is completed.
